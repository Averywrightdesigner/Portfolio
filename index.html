About me

hi, nice to meet you!

I'm Avery, a UI/UX designer from Kennesaw, Georgia. I graduated from Kennesaw State University in 2026 with a B.S. in Interactive Design.

My favorite part of design is the work most people never see: talking to users, sorting through research, mapping out flows, and testing prototypes until they make sense to the people who will use them.

Before design, I spent over five years working in coffee shops and restaurants. Running a busy Starbucks bar and serving tables taught me how to figure out what people need, and how to stay calm when things get hectic. I use both of those skills every day as a designer.

I'm looking for a full-time role where I can keep learning from a great team and design things that help people.
