The problem

Students deserve a safe online space for regulating their mental health that is both secure and private.

The tools that already existed were either too complex or locked behind paywalls.

Leading the team

I pitched the idea and gathered a team of six talented people to help bring Nessie to life, then led the project as Project Manager.

Research

Every good project starts with research. Team Nessie completed:

  1. Problem identification
  2. Competitive research against similar products on the market
  3. A literature review

The app

Nessie gives students simple, private tools for checking in with themselves and finding support.

  • Mood calendar
  • Journaling
  • Breathing techniques
  • Community support
